About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Fudan University, supervised by Yunlei Zhao. I have also been advised by Haodong Jiang since 2024. I expect to graduate in July 2027.

I am broadly interested in cryptography, particularly the design and security analysis of cryptographic primitives and protocols for the post-quantum era.

Research interests

  • Post-quantum & hybrid cryptographyKEM constructions, authenticated key exchange, and forward secrecy.
  • Cryptographic protocol analysisSecurity models and modular analyses for TLS 1.3 and KEM-based handshakes.
  • Provable securitySecurity analyses in the random oracle model (ROM) and quantum random oracle model (QROM).
