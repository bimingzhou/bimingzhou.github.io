## About Me

My name is **Biming Zhou (周铋洺)**. I am a fourth-year PhD student at Fudan University, supervised by **[Yunlei Zhao](https://cs.fudan.edu.cn/3f/ba/c25909a278458/page.htm)**. I have also been fortunate to work under the guidance of **Haodong Jiang** since 2024. I expect to graduate in July 2027.

My research focuses on post-quantum cryptography, particularly post-quantum authenticated key exchange (AKE), key encapsulation mechanisms (KEMs), and their applications to real-world protocols such as TLS 1.3.
